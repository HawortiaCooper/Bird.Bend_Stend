0. Safety rules (read before you touch anything)

These rules are the constraints of the PO-approved bench procedure (FW_test_plan §6.8 P-1, P-7; D-45) applied to the bring-up. They are not negotiable.

  • ☐ 0.1 The red E-stop is not an IEC 60204-1 emergency stop (D-41). It stops the pulses in the firmware and cuts the driver's ENA input directly through a second contact (D-42), but the 48 V supply stays on. The only power removal is the mains switch of the 48 V PSU. Keep both — red button and PSU switch — within reach whenever the 48 V is on.
  • ☐ 0.2 Two persons whenever the driver is powered (§6.8 P-1): the operator at the PC and the observer at the operator panel with a hand near the red button and the PSU switch.
  • ☐ 0.3 No specimen, no load on the table, no tool in the travel for the whole bring-up (§6.8 P-1). The only load is the calibration weights hanging on the load cell in section 11, with the axis standing still and PAUSED.
  • ☐ 0.4 Hands clear of the ball screw, the table and the load path while the driver is powered. The motor is energised as soon as the 48 V is on (the driver holds even with the board reset, D-13). Touch the coupling only in the steps that ask for it, and only after the operator has said "hands off the mouse". Hands off the shaft before the E-stop or the RESET button is released and before any Clear / ENABLE — the closed-loop driver re-energises at once.
  • ☐ 0.5 First motion is slow: jogs at 1 mm/s (the firmware caps un-homed motion at 2 mm/s), homing at the firmware's homing speed (≤ 5 mm/s), every Go to in this guide at 5 mm/s (never above 10 mm/s). The only exception is the pulse-integrity check 10.2 at 30 mm/s (= the §6.8 P-1 limit; no specimen, nothing in the travel, the observer's hand at the red E-stop).
  • ☐ 0.6 Never bypass the E-stop sense input (no jumper, no stimulus, no unplugged sense connector while the driver is powered). The §6.8 bypass is part of the HW gate only.
  • ☐ 0.7 Abort criteria (§6.8 P-7): any unexpected motion, noise, smell, a driver alarm (ALM red with 48 V on), or the table outside the expected position → the observer presses the red E-stop at once (independent D-42 path) and switches the PSU off; the operator releases any jog button and presses STOP. Write an incident note (annex B).
  • ☐ 0.8 Wiring changes only with everything off: PSU mains switch off and Nucleo USB unplugged. The driver DIP switches are changed only with the driver unpowered.
  • ☐ 0.9 Never mount a specimen while the amber banner NO-SPECIMEN MODE … is shown (USER_MANUAL §5).
  • ☐ 0.10 Start only when the PO has released this board for bring-up (D-06: flashing and opening the COM port need the PO's go). Record the PO's release (name, date) in annex B.

3. Wiring check with all power OFF

Preconditions: 48 V PSU mains switch off, Nucleo USB unplugged, E-stop released. Use the diagram sheets.

3.1 Visual inspection (≈ HG-01 / C-01, HG-20 / C-20)

  • ☐ 3.1.1 Nucleo NUCLEO-F446RE (MB1136): solder bridges as sheet 2 box "Solder bridges": SB13, SB14 ON · SB62, SB63 OFF · SB16, SB50 ON · SB54, SB55 OFF · SB51, SB56 ON · SB46, SB52 OFF; JP5 = U5V (USB power). Photo of both sides.
  • ☐ 3.1.2 Nothing is wired to D0 / D1 (PA3 / PA2 are the PC link).
  • ☐ 3.1.3 Every connection of sheet 2 is present at the pin given there (Arduino name and CN number — the CN numbers are ASSUMED from the Nucleo manual: compare with the board silkscreen): PUL A0 · DIR A1 · ENA A2 (through D1) · START A3 · END A4 · ALM D7 · PEND D8 · E-stop sense D2 · PAUSE D10 · HX711 DOUT D5 · PD_SCK D6 (33 Ω) · RATE D4.
  • ☐ 3.1.4 Driver "Signal" terminals: PUL− , DIR− , ENA− go to the Nucleo GND (common cathode); "OUT": ALM− , PEND− to Nucleo GND (sheet 4).
  • ☐ 3.1.5 E-stop circuit as sheet 3 "ENA: MCU drive OR hardwired E-stop cut": NC block → PA10; NO block → +5V_CUT → R_E 68 Ω → D2 → ENA+; D1 between PA4 and ENA+; 10 kΩ ENA+ → GND; no contactor, nothing from the MCU in the NO path; a label "not an IEC 60204-1 emergency stop" on the panel.
  • ☐ 3.1.6 The +5V_CUT DC-DC is fed from the 48 V bus after F1 (sheet 1); F1 = T10A fitted.
  • ☐ 3.1.7 Cable routing: control cable and load-cell cable ≥ 10 cm from motor / 48 V wires, crossings at 90°.

3.3 Limit-switch positions, by hand (≈ HG-25 travel part, A-07)

With all power off the motor is free: move the table slowly by turning the coupling / screw by hand.

  • ☐ 3.3.1 Move the table toward the START end with the multimeter on 3.2-1 (continuity): the START switch must open (≈ 0 Ω → OL) at least 3 mm before the table reaches any mechanical stop (end block, nut end, coupling). Measure the remaining distance to the stop.
  • ☐ 3.3.2 Same at the END end with 3.2-2: the END switch opens ≥ 5 mm before any mechanical stop.
  • ☐ 3.3.3 Measure the switch-to-switch span (table position where START opens → where END opens): ≥ 295 mm.
  • ☐ 3.3.4 Write the two margins and the span into annex B; leave the table near the middle (≥ 20 mm from both switches).
  • Margin < 3 mm, a switch that does not open, or a span < 295 mm → STOP and adjust the switch mounting.

5. Power-up order and flashing

  • ☐ 5.1 48 V PSU stays OFF for sections 5–7.
  • ☐ 5.2 E-stop released; START / END switches not actuated; nothing in the travel.
  • ☐ 5.3 Plug the Nucleo into the PC by USB. Expected: the Nucleo power LED is on, the ST-LINK COM LED (LD1) lights, Windows shows the ST-LINK drive and a COM port STLink Virtual COM Port (COMn) (Device Manager → Ports). Write COMn into annex B. If not → STOP (FLASHING §6).
  • ☐ 5.4 Check the file hash and flash BBS_FW_0.8.6_release.bin by drag-and-drop — method A of FLASHING (first bring-up; the saved configuration is wiped on purpose — the board still holds Stefan's old program).
  • ☐ 5.5 After flashing: no FAIL.TXT; the green LED LD2 shows slow blink (1 s on / 1 s off: running, no latch — also with one limit switch open) or double blink (a latch: HALT / PAUSED / ESTOP / a FAULT, e.g. the E-stop pressed or LIMIT_WIRING with both limit connectors open). Triple blink = fallback clock → STOP (solder bridges SB16 / SB50). LD2 dark or steady → STOP.
  • Later updates of this board: method B2 (CubeProgrammer + .hex, no full chip erase) — it keeps the saved configuration (FLASHING §1).

7. Input checks — 48 V still OFF (≈ HG-17 partly, HG-32)

  • ☐ 7.1 E-stop: press the red button. Expected: ESTOP red ESTOP, red stop banner (E-stop latched), LD2 double-blinks. Keep it pressed > 1 s: only ESTOP is latched — no K1_WELDED, no other fault (≈ HG-32). Optional DMM (M-6): PA10 pin (CN10-33 / D2) ≥ 2.3 V pressed, ≤ 0.4 V released.
  • ☐ 7.2 Release the button: ESTOP stays red (latched). Wait ≥ 100 ms → Clear stop… → tick E-stop button released, area safe → Clear E-STOP → ESTOP green. (Clearing never moves anything.)
  • ☐ 7.3 PAUSE: press and release once → PAUSED amber PAUSED BUTTON, banner PAUSED (BUTTON) – motion blocked. with Resume; BTN amber PAUSE pressed while the button is held. Clear with ▶ Resume (toolbar), the banner's Resume, or by pressing PAUSE again → PAUSED green.
  • ☐ 7.4 START switch: press the lever by hand → LIM S red LIMIT_START; release → green again after ≈ 20 ms.
  • ☐ 7.5 END switch: same → LIM E red LIMIT_END; release → green.
  • ☐ 7.6 Wire-break test (≈ HG-17): unplug the START connector → LIM S red LIMIT_START (LD2 keeps the slow blink); plug back → green. Same for END.
  • ☐ 7.7 Both connectors unplugged at once → LIM S red LIMIT_START LIMIT_WIRING, LIM E red LIMIT_END LIMIT_WIRING, FAULT red (LD2 double blink). Plug both back → Clear stop… → Clear faults → all green.
  • ☐ 7.8 ALM red ALM – new motion blocked (driver unpowered) — note it, expected.
  • If any input does not react as written → STOP (wiring of that input, sheet 2 / sheet 3).

8. 48 V on: driver power, ENABLE / DISABLE, the D-42 ENA-cut check

Preconditions: sections 3–7 ticked; two persons in place; nothing in the travel; Driver enabled (Manual tab) unticked; E-stop released.

  • ☐ 8.1 Press the black RESET button (B2) of the Nucleo once (the E-stop test of section 7 left ENA at disabled; after a restart the board leaves the driver holding again, D-13). The amber banner LINK LOST – reconnecting… appears for a moment; the PC reconnects; ESTOP green.
  • ☐ 8.2 Observer switches the 48 V PSU on. Expected: driver power LED on, no alarm LED; the motor holds (energised — the board leaves the driver holding after boot, D-13, although the ENA chip says disabled).
  • ☐ 8.3 Within ≈ 1 s ALM turns green OFF; PEND in position. ALM stays red → STOP: driver alarm or the PFDE clone uses the other ALM polarity (drv.alm_active_level, decided at HG-16 by the maintainer). Record what the driver LEDs show. (≈ HG-16)
  • ☐ 8.4 E-stop with the board running: observer presses the red button → ESTOP latched; the operator (hands off the mouse) turns the coupling / screw gently by hand: it turns freely (de-energised). Hands off the shaft, then the observer releases the button → still free (check again, hands off afterwards). Clear stop… → Clear E-STOP → still free.
  • ☐ 8.5 ENABLE (≈ HG-07): hands off the shaft → Manual tab → tick Driver enabled → ENA amber ENABLING … ms, then green enabled (≈ 0.5 s) → the motor holds again.
  • ☐ 8.6 DISABLE = free — the MCU ENA path on its own (≈ HG-07, C-07): E-stop released; untick Driver enabled → dialog Disable the driver: tick Specimen is unloaded → Disable (Enter does not confirm). ENA red disabled. The operator turns the coupling: it must turn freely. Holds → STOP: the MCU does not drive ENA to disabled — check the ENA drive / D1 / I_LED per sheet 3 before any motion. Hands off → tick Driver enabled again → ENABLING … ms → enabled → holds.
  • ☐ 8.7 D-42 hardwired ENA cut, MCU in reset (≈ HG-10 c / C-25 — the periodic check of D-54 e). Roles: the observer holds RESET and presses the E-stop, the operator turns the shaft.
    1. observer holds the black RESET button (B2) of the Nucleo pressed (the MCU is in reset; the PC shows the amber banner LINK LOST – reconnecting… — normal);
    2. observer presses the red E-stop → the operator turns the shaft: it must turn freely. If it holds → STOP: the hardwired cut does not work — no motion until it is fixed (§6.8 P-2 logic);
    3. operator: hands off the shaft; observer releases the E-stop while still holding RESET → the shaft holds again (the 10 kΩ pull-down lets the driver enable, no pulses are possible — expected, D-45 a);
    4. observer releases RESET → the board restarts: ENA disabled (the motor keeps holding, D-13), HOMED NOT homed, link back.
  • ☐ 8.8 Optional DMM on the ENA loop (≈ HG-10 d; sheet 3; clip leads, a third hand needed for RESET): M-1 across R_E ≈ 0.7 V (MCU in reset + E-stop pressed) · M-4 ENA+ ≈ 4.0…4.6 V pressed, ≈ 0 V released + enabled · M-3 PA4 (CN8-3 / A2) never above 3.4 V · M-5 +5V_CUT 4.75…5.25 V. Values into annex B.
  • ☐ 8.9 Driver alarm reset (only if an ALM appears, USER_MANUAL §17, D-54 d): E-stop → 48 V off, wait until the driver LEDs are dark → 48 V on → release E-stop → Clear E-STOP → Driver enabled → HOME.

9. First motion — slow, no specimen (≈ HG-28, HG-11 real-actuation part)

Preconditions: section 8 complete, the D-42 check and the DISABLE-free check passed, two persons, nothing in the travel, table near the middle (section 3.3). Motion parameters → speed = 5 mm/s for every Go to in this guide (never above 10 mm/s). If the table does not stop where this section expects it: the observer presses the red E-stop at once (independent D-42 path); the operator releases the jog button and presses STOP → STOP, report.

  • ☐ 9.1 No-specimen mode (USER_MANUAL §5; without calibration all motion is refused): Safety limits tab → Enter no-specimen mode… → tick No specimen is mounted → Switch PC load limits off. Expected: amber banner NO-SPECIMEN MODE – PC load limits OFF for this session …, NOSPEC chip. The board load limit stays active.
  • ☐ 9.2 Manual tab: Driver enabled ticked; jog speed = 1 mm/s.
  • ☐ 9.3 Direction: note Travel (machine); zero the caliper between table and frame. Hold hold forward (+) ▶▶ for ≈ 3 s, release. Expected: the table moved away from the START switch and stopped on release. Toward START → STOP: record; with the PO's agreement set motion.dir_invert (Connection & Config, Edit → Write & verify) and repeat; it is saved in section 12.
  • ☐ 9.4 Scale: caliper distance = change of Travel (machine) within ± 10 %. About 5 × the shown change → the DIP switches SW1–SW4 are still at 800 pulses/rev → STOP, 48 V off, section 4. Record both values.
  • ☐ 9.5 START switch stop, before the first HOME (un-homed; the PC travel limits do not act on un-homed jogs): hold ◀◀ rear (−) hold at 1 mm/s until the table reaches the START switch (this may take a few minutes; release and press again as needed). Expected: the jog itself is stopped by the switch (event STOPPED LIMIT_START — pressing the lever by hand does not count), LIM S red LIMIT_START; a further (−) jog is refused; hold forward (+) ▶▶ moves away and LIM S clears (green).
  • ☐ 9.6 HOME: press HOME; the application asks first (unknown load) → tick I accept homing under load → Home. Expected: the table drives to the START switch at low speed, backs off, HOMED green homed, Axis box ● HOMED. Moves away from START, or HOME_WIRING / HOME_NOT_FOUND / LIM E → E-stop / STOP as above, report.
  • ☐ 9.7 Homed move check: zero the caliper, +10 (Steps) → the caliper shows 10.00 mm ± 0.5 mm; Go to absolute 20 mm → Go.
  • ☐ 9.8 END switch stop at low speed (real actuation): Go to absolute 280 mm (5 mm/s). Untick Driver enabled (dialog Disable the driver: tick Specimen is unloaded → Disable) and — hands off — tick it again (ENABLING … ms → enabled; the axis is now un-homed, so the 290 mm soft limit no longer stops the jog; the PC travel limits do not act un-homed either). Jog hold forward (+) ▶▶ at 1 mm/s until the table reaches the END switch. Expected: the jog itself is stopped by the switch (event STOPPED LIMIT_END), LIM E red LIMIT_END; a further (+) jog is refused; ◀◀ rear (−) hold moves away and LIM E clears.
  • ☐ 9.9 HOME again (as above). Keep the no-specimen mode on for section 10.

10. Direct-drive qualification (SYS-011, D-56 b) — before any calibration or test campaign

No buffer board is fitted (D-56 b): the driver inputs stay on the direct 3.3 V drive, which is below the driver's 5–24 V rating. Before calibration and before every test campaign it is qualified here (wiring.md §2.2), with the release image only. The SN74ACT244 buffer board is needed only if 10.1 or 10.2 fails. Section 10.2 is repeated after any wiring change and at the end of every test campaign. Known limitation: a pulse the driver never saw during a test is found only by the end-of-campaign re-home (10.2).

10.1 Opto current I_LED on ENA and DIR (≈ C-06 / HG-06 ENA and DIR part, M-2 / M-8)

  • ☐ 10.1.1 Everything off (PSU off, USB unplugged): insert a 100 Ω 1 % shunt in series with ENA− (between the driver ENA− terminal and logic GND). Label it. (The DIR shunt comes in 10.1.3 — never both in the loop while the motor is powered: the ENA shunt weakens the D-42 cut.)
  • ☐ 10.1.2 USB in, Connect; the 48 V stays OFF for the ENA reading (USB only).
  • ☐ 10.1.3 ENA (48 V off): press the red E-stop and release it → Clear stop… → Clear E-STOP. ENA now stays at the disabled level driven by the MCU alone (SAF-FW-006; after a plain boot it would be at the holding level and read 0 — a false FAIL). DMM across the ENA shunt ≥ 0.60 V (= I_LED ≥ 6 mA; 0.45…0.60 V → repeat in 10.1.5); write it into annex B. Then everything off (USB unplugged), remove the ENA shunt (ENA− back to logic GND), insert the 100 Ω shunt in series with DIR−, label it.
  • ☐ 10.1.4 DIR (48 V on): USB in, Connect, observer switches the 48 V on, re-enter the no-specimen mode (as 9.1 — the power cycle ended it), Driver enabled (hands off), table mid-stroke, ≥ 20 mm from both switches (a wrong DIR reading must never drive the table into a switch). The board keeps DIR at the level of the last move direction (high = LED current after a (+) move with motion.dir_invert = 0; after a (−) move if it is 1). Jog hold forward (+) ▶▶ at 1 mm/s for ≈ 1 s (dir_invert = 1: ◀◀ rear (−) hold), release: DMM across the DIR shunt ≥ 0.60 V (0.45…0.60 V → repeat in 10.1.5). A short jog the other way brings it to ≈ 0 V (proves the shunt is in the DIR loop).
  • ☐ 10.1.5 Write the DIR reading into annex B. Repeat decision (always answer): all readings ≥ 0.60 V → no repeat; a reading of 0.45…0.60 V → the 100 Ω shunt itself lowers the current by about a quarter — repeat that input with the DMM mA range in series (burden ≤ 10 Ω) or a 10 Ω shunt (≥ 60 mV); that reading decides and must be ≥ 6 mA (else STOP). The assistant refuses "no repeat" while a 10.1.3 / 10.1.4 reading is below 0.60 V or missing, and then asks for the mA values.
  • ☐ 10.1.6 Everything off, remove the DIR shunt, restore DIR− to logic GND (photo of ENA− and DIR− restored).
  • Wiring changes (shunts) only with the PSU off and the USB unplugged.
  • < 6 mA on ENA or DIR → STOP: the buffer board is required (D-56 b) — report to the PO. PUL cannot be held at a static level with the release image; its path is qualified by 10.2.

10.2 Pulse-integrity check (≈ HG-09 / HG-25 related; FW-HOM-003 / FW-HOM-004)

Numbers (SYS-011, SRS v0.6.8): stroke 5 mm ↔ 285 mm (280 mm inside the 0.5 / 290 mm soft limits), N = 10 round trips (20 moves, 5.6 m, ≈ 4.5 million steps at 800 steps/mm), at 30 mm/s (fixed; = the default motion.v_max_travel_um_s, 24 kHz; the check never runs faster). Baseline: 3 re-homes of the homed axis (d0₁…d0₃). Pass: no HOME_DRIFT and |d1| ≤ max(30 µm, 2 · max|d0ᵢ|) (d is the difference of two switch captures, so the switch's own scatter sets the limit; the HOME_DRIFT latch at 200 µm, home.drift_tol_um, is the hard limit); one repeat allowed. This is the only step of this guide faster than 10 mm/s: no specimen, nothing in the travel, the observer's hand at the red E-stop. Run it only after 9.5 and 9.8 (real START / END stops) passed the same day.

  • ☐ 10.2.1 USB / 48 V on; re-enter the no-specimen mode (as 9.1), Driver enabled, HOME (as 9.6) — the 10.1 power cycles left the axis un-homed and ended the mode; View ▸ Event log open.
  • ☐ 10.2.2 Baseline: the axis is homed (HOMED green). Press HOME three more times — each runs on a homed axis; in the event log each line HOMED shows values d / … (d = deviation in µm, no values shown = 0): write d0₁, d0₂, d0₃ into annex B and compute the limit max(30 µm, 2 · max|d0ᵢ|). max|d0ᵢ| > 100 µm → STOP: check the START switch mounting (switch repeatability, not a drive fault; the limit would reach the 200 µm HOME_DRIFT latch).
  • ☐ 10.2.3 Moves (in the assistant: run by 10.2.2, this step shows its verdict; paper procedure: USER_MANUAL §15): Sequence tab → New; Settings: travel ref = machine; + Step ▾ travel: Target 285, v mm/s 30; + Step ▾ travel: Target 5, v mm/s 30; select both rows → Loop… count 10 → ▶ Start (confirm the travel-only start in the no-specimen mode). Expected: 20 moves, sequence FINISHED, no ALM, no stop. (Alternative: Manual tab, Motion parameters speed 30, Go to absolute 285 / 5 twenty times.) A refusal E_RANGE "speed above cap" / "loaded" → STOP: write down the Raw readout and report; never raise a speed cap (before a tare safety.zero_raw = 0 and the 2 % FS band decides "loaded").
  • ☐ 10.2.4 Re-home: check that HOMED is green immediately before pressing HOME (otherwise the HOME is a first homing and d = 0 means nothing). Any E-stop, Disable, ALM, LINK LOST or fault during 10.2.3 → repeat from 10.2.2. HOME → event HOMED values d1 / …. Pass: no HOME_DRIFT (FAULT chip green) and |d1| ≤ max(30 µm, 2 · max|d0ᵢ|). Write d1 into annex B.
  • ☐ 10.2.5 Fail (|d1| above the limit, or HOME_DRIFT latched — Clear faults clears it) → repeat 10.2 once from 10.2.2; fails again → STOP: the buffer board is required (D-56 b) — report to the PO.
  • ☐ 10.2.6 Repeat this check after any wiring change and at the end of every test campaign (SYS-011).
  • ☐ 10.2.7 Leave no-specimen mode (banner or Safety limits tab).
